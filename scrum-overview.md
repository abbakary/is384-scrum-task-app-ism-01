Scrum Roles

Our Scrum team is organized as follows:

Product Owner
Oversees the application vision and decides which features bring the highest value to users. This role is responsible for prioritizing requirements and clarifying expectations.

Scrum Master
Facilitates Scrum activities, ensures that the team follows Agile principles, and helps resolve any challenges affecting progress.

Development Team
Responsible for designing and preparing the task management features. Developers collaborate to deliver functional components within the sprint timeframe.
